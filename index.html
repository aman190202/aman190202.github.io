I'm a master's student in computer science at Brown University, specializing in visual computing and AI, advised by Dr. James Tompkin.

My research is driven by the goal of building tools that geo-localize captured images and multimodal data using 3D geometric cues, with the intention of supporting investigative journalism.

More broadly, I enjoy building systems that induce spatial intelligence from 2D visual data. I am a founding engineer at InVision Labs, where we are developing tools for creating digital twins from smartphone-captured videos.
